About Zephyr

A clean, fast job board for remote tech roles

What is Zephyr?

Zephyr is a curated job board focused exclusively on remote technology roles. We believe great work happens everywhere — not just in a handful of coastal cities. Our goal is to make it simple to discover opportunities at companies that embrace remote-first culture.

Why Zephyr?

Most job boards bury remote filters behind signup walls, mix in low-quality listings, or make you scroll past irrelevant results. Zephyr puts powerful filters front and center: search by title, company, tech stack, remote type, and salary range — all on one page, with no accounts required.

How it works

  1. Browse — Scroll remote tech jobs from 50+ companies.
  2. Filter — Narrow results by title, company, tech stack, remote type, or salary.
  3. Apply — Click through to the company's career page and submit your application.
